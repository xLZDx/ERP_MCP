What the reviews found and what was fixed

  • S8 review round (code 080c76d; four read-only reviewers): 0 BLOCKER, 2 HIGH, about 10 MAJOR; fixed in one batch, 5f51d0f.
  • Main points: company isolation was missing on the cards, annotations, rerun and job enqueue paths; the viewer scope was assembled by the caller; a rerun created its run before the job was dispatched (a retry gave a false STALE); global quotas let one tenant starve the others; attestations in the timeline leaked across companies.
  • Process: some streams wrote code before tests (red tests were observed only partly: G3 29 of 36), recorded in the decision log.
  • GPT-PM: pending. The S7 request is stuck on a PM Bridge browser failure (CHATGPT_RENDERER_STALLED); a forced restart is an operator break-glass and was not used.
