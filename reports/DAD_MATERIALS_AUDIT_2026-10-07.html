3. The DAD month-close report against the base copy

The DAD report was built from records extracted from the base. Against the 1C engine's turnovers and balances, accounts 216.1, 241.1, 611.1, 711.1, 821 and 521.1 agree. Account 811.1 does not agree (August turnover, opening and closing balance) and the total August turnover differs too. The cause is not established. Hypotheses: period boundaries and time zone, or the active-record filter. Product conclusion: totals must not be rebuilt from posting rows; balance-and-turnover virtual tables are needed, as in `accounting_balance_and_turnovers`.

5. Gaps found in this review

A. Corrected after verification. This item used to say that analytics per counterparty is unreachable: that was wrong, only the entity list had been checked, not the functions. On the published OData the accounting register functions Balance and RecordsWithExtDimensions work and return analytics (counterparty, contract) and balances by it. What is missing is a tool: the current accounting_balance_and_turnovers returns seven canonical fields without analytics. Operator decision: a hybrid, OData as the primary route and COM as the fallback for bases where analytics is not published.
B. The DAD report disagrees with the engine on account 811.1 and on the total turnover; the cause is not found (section 3). Until it is explained, nothing can be said about the 811.1 balance for August.
C. Checks by item and warehouse (211) and by day (241) need queries with analytics and by day. They were not run on the real copy yet. This is the next step for NR-05..NR-07.
D. Native UI reports from the accountant are still needed for the ten cases; engine queries are comparison only and do not replace them.
E. The source document contains a plaintext login and password of the test copy. In ERP_MCP it is a secret reference only; the password must not be copied into Git, reports or prompts. Rotating it is recommended if the copy is used more widely.

6. Recommendation for payable_balance

  1. Do not look for a settlements accumulation register: this configuration has none.
  2. Define payable_balance through an account-based profile: 521 as debt, 224 and 544 as advances and other. The approved profile owns the account numbers, not the code.
  3. The account total is already confirmed: the gateway answer equals the engine query for 521.1.
  4. Take the per-supplier split from register balances of account 521 with the counterparty analytics (OData Balance, COM fallback), not from the sum of receipt documents: offsets and prepayments are invisible that way.
  5. Keep debt aging unavailable. Acceptance: a trial balance for 521 from the 1C interface prepared by the accountant.
