## 2026-10-09 - D-013: review of the resnapshot / validated-resume follow-up (94e2605) and its fixes

A narrow code-reviewer read 94e2605: 0 BLOCKER, 2 MAJOR, 6 MINOR. Both MAJOR were reproduced in the code and fixed. (M1) ResnapshotTracker.complete had no ordering fence: a late or replayed complete whose capture began before the cursor loss was recorded (or whose epoch was older than the recorded one) could clear the requirement.
