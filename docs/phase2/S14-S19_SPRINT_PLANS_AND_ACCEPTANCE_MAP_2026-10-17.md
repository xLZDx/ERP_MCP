## Ordered execution and stop conditions

1. Finish S13 source work and its designated tests/reviews. Keep local lifecycle status fail-closed until shared admission, recovery, writer coverage and provider requirements are satisfied.
2. Complete S14 only for channel/operation pairs measured and approved by CT14. If the licensed writer boundary is unavailable, complete the local protocol/simulation and record real COM evidence `NOT_RUN`; do not label S14 fully accepted.
3. Complete S15, then S16, then S17. S18 follows S13–S16 and may overlap S17 except its intake-specific security review. S19 follows S17/S18.
4. Every sprint closes only against its canonical story IDs and listed evidence. After code is complete, `functional-test-reviewer` and `e2e-runner` own tests. Relevant specialist agents review the exact final diff; GPT checks the complete evidence and approves an exact HEAD before push/PR.
5. G1/G2/G3/G5/G6-pre and any enabled G4 evidence remain independent external gate work. G6 canary is considered only after its prerequisites; G7/Release 2 stays NO-GO until exact `PRODUCTION_GO` is recorded.
