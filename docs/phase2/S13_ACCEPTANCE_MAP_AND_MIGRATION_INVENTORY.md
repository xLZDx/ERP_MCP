Reliability review found that the timeout setting persisted for the remainder of the transaction; additive migration 024 restores the caller's prior setting after lock acquisition or error. CR145 records functional and E2E PASS on disposable PostgreSQL for migrations 001–024; DB, reliability, and security reviews at exact evidence HEAD `3a5f71d` found no BLOCKER/MAJOR in the changed local scope. DB review qualifies that the savepoint test proves the outer transaction state after rollback but does not isolate restoration performed by the function's exception handler. The reliability review identifies a separate MAJOR acceptance gap before S13 activation: a successful `FOR SHARE` row lock lasts until the transaction ends, and no bounded service transaction lifetime is demonstrated. The physical writer overlap finding remains unresolved because no real writer/service admission integration exists, and no migration exposes a READY transition. Keep 022–024 unapplied outside disposable evidence.
